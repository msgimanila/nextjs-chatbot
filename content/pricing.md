## Pricing plans

We offer three plans: Starter ($19/month), Pro ($49/month), and Enterprise
(custom pricing). All plans include a 14-day free trial and can be canceled
at any time from your account settings.

## What's included in the Pro plan?

The Pro plan includes unlimited projects, priority email support, and
access to advanced analytics. It's billed monthly or annually, with a
discount for annual billing.
