## What is this product?

This is a demo site showing a simple support chatbot that answers common
questions using content stored right in this project, with an AI fallback
for anything not covered.

## What are your business hours?

We're available Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm Eastern Time. Messages
sent outside these hours are answered the next business day.

## How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click "Forgot password." Enter your email address
and you'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. If it doesn't arrive,
check your spam folder before contacting support.

## Do you offer refunds?

Yes. We offer a full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked.
Contact support with your order number to start the process.

## How do I contact support?

Email support@example.com or use the chat widget on this page. We typically
reply within one business day.
