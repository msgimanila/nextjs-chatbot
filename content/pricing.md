

# IntellicaAI Pricing — Frequently Asked Questions

## Understanding IntellicaAI Pricing

### 1. How much does IntellicaAI cost?

IntellicaAI plans start at **$120/month**. You can choose from four main plans:

* **Explorer — $120/month**
* **Builder — $179/month**
* **Workforce — $299/month**
* **Business — $449/month**

For larger organizations, **Enterprise starts around $799/month**.

### 2. What am I paying for with IntellicaAI?

You're paying for an AI workforce that can perform real business work—not simply access to a chatbot.

Your plan provides AI Workers, work capacity, app connections, automation capabilities, memory, and other features depending on the plan you choose.

### 3. What is an AI Worker?

An AI Worker is a digital worker that can perform business tasks such as answering emails, following up with leads, managing schedules, researching information, handling documents, and carrying out automated workflows.

Think of an AI Worker as a digital employee that can work alongside your human team.

### 4. Do I pay for each AI Worker?

No. **Every IntellicaAI plan lets you build and try unlimited AI Workers.**

Your plan is primarily based on the amount of work your AI Workers can perform each month and the capabilities available to them.

### 5. What does "unlimited AI Workers" mean?

You can create and experiment with as many AI Workers as you need within the platform.

Your subscription isn't based on buying a separate subscription for every AI Worker. Instead, your plan determines the available work capacity and capabilities.

### 6. What is a Work Unit?

A **Work Unit** represents the amount of work an AI Worker can perform.

The simplest way to think about it is like **hours on a timesheet**: the more work capacity your plan has, the more work your AI Workers can handle.

### 7. What is a Workspace?

A **Workspace** is a separate environment for organizing a project, business, team, or department.

It helps keep different teams or operations separated and organized.

### 8. What are App Connections?

App connections allow your AI Workers to work with the business tools you already use.

Depending on the configuration, this can include tools such as:

* Email
* Calendars
* CRM systems
* Business applications
* Databases
* Automation platforms
* Other connected services

### 9. What are Multi-Agent Workflows?

A multi-agent workflow allows several AI Workers to work together on the same business process.

For example, one AI Worker could research a lead, another could prepare the information, and another could follow up.

Think of it as **a small AI department working together**.

### 10. What is Human Approval?

Human approval allows a real person to review and approve an action before it happens.

This is useful for tasks where you want human oversight—for example, before an important message, document, or business action is sent.
